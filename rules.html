⚡ The Awkward Game — Rules

The Premise

Roll, move, land on a tile, face the awkwardness, get judged by your friends. Collect Awkward Points (good) while dodging Shame Points (bad). Highest AP wins; lowest SP breaks ties.

Turn Flow

  1. Roll the die (1–6).
  2. Move that many tiles. Crossing START grants +2 AP.
  3. Resolve the tile — its type decides which deck you draw from.
  4. Respond: do it, bluff it, choose, take the risk, or sabotage.
  5. Get judged: some cards trigger a group vote (Believe/Caught, Forgive/Punish).
  6. Apply the effects, next player rolls.

Tile Types (54 tiles)

  • Scenario (13) — awkward situations; do it for AP, refuse for +1 SP, or bluff.
  • Choice (9) — two awful options, pick one or eat 3 SP.
  • Risk (8) — gamble (coin flip / roll 4+ / group believes you) or chicken out for 1 SP.
  • Sabotage (6) — spend a Sabotage Token 🎟️ on another player, or skip for +1 AP.
  • Group Vote (5) — the table judges you: Forgive or Punish.
  • Safe (7) — nothing happens. Bliss.
  • Chaos (3) — random deck draw, +1 Sabotage Token.
  • Double Trouble (2) — Scenario + Choice back to back, +1 Sabotage Token.
  • Final Combo (1) — Scenario + Choice + Risk; survive all three for +10 AP.

Card Types (180 cards)

  • Scenario (36) — do it (+AP), refuse (+SP), or bluff (group votes Believe/Caught; caught = SP).
  • Choice (36) — option A or B, each with its own AP/SP.
  • Risk (36) — coin / die / believe mechanics, reward vs penalty.
  • Sabotage (36) — token cost; effects hit a target of your choice.
  • Group Vote (36) — majority rules (tie forgives) or unanimous mercy.

AI Personalities

Chill, Chaotic, Brutal, Honest and Liar each have different odds for bluffing, risk-taking, sabotage and punishment. Brutal hunts the leader. Honest never bluffs (barring chaos rolls). The Liar gets caught constantly and it's wonderful.

Multiplayer

One player creates a room and shares the 6-character code. Everyone joins from their own device. The server holds the deck order and validates every move; clients only receive what they're allowed to know.

House Rules

Anyone may call the safe word and skip a card for +1 SP, no questions asked. The game rewards playing along with the bit — never actual distress.
